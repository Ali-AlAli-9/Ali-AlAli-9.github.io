Smart Solar Tracker

Sugeno fuzzy logic-based solar panel tracking system. Designed and modeled in MATLAB/Simulink with a custom 15-rule fuzzy inference engine, then auto-generated to C via Embedded Coder and deployed on Arduino Uno. Hand-optimized Arduino sketch with no external libraries featuring LDR-based light sensing, low-pass filtering, and smooth-step servo control — reducing response time from 9.5s to 3s.
